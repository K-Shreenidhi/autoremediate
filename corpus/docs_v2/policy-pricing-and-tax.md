# Pricing and tax policy

## Rounding and order of operations
All amounts are integer cents. A fraction of a cent is rounded to the nearest cent, and an exact half always rounds up (1.5 cents becomes 2 cents). Amounts are never truncated and never rounded to even.

## Coupons and tax
A coupon takes a percentage off the subtotal first. Sales tax of 7.5 percent is then calculated on the discounted subtotal, never on the original subtotal.
