# Reporting policy

## Reporting day
The store runs in India. The reporting day follows local store time, UTC+05:30 (five hours thirty minutes ahead of UTC), not UTC. An order placed at 18:45 UTC on 1 January belongs to 2 January.

## What counts as revenue
Revenue is the total paid for orders that are not cancelled. Every order has a creation timestamp that the system sets when the order is created.
